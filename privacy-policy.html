BosKira privacy policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

BosKira keeps your financial information on your phone. It has no internet access, has no accounts, and doesn't send your data to us or anyone else.

What BosKira reads

With your permission (Android's "notification access"), BosKira reads notifications from the OCBC app, Google Wallet and Touch 'n Go eWallet so it can record your payments automatically. From those notifications it uses the amount, the shop or person paid, and the time.

OCBC sends some alerts as text messages. BosKira sees those through your messaging app's notification and keeps only messages from OCBC that mention an amount. One-time passwords and all other messages are dropped before anything is saved. BosKira never opens your SMS inbox.

If you use another bank, you can choose to switch on its app in BosKira's Capture mode. BosKira then also saves that app's notifications, so you can export them and help BosKira learn to read them. Apps you don't switch on are ignored.

Some banks send the amount only by email. If you turn on Bank emails, BosKira looks at the new-mail notifications from Gmail or Samsung Email: the sender's name and the preview lines shown in the notification. It keeps only emails whose sender is a bank or e-wallet and whose preview mentions an amount, and drops any with a code or password. Every other email is ignored and nothing about it is saved. BosKira never opens your mailbox. Bank emails are off until you turn them on.

If you split a bill by its receipt, BosKira reads the photo you choose on this phone to find the items and prices. The photo isn't copied or kept.

Android delivers every notification to apps with notification access. BosKira ignores notifications from all other apps, every message in your messaging app that isn't from OCBC, and every email that isn't a bank's money alert: it doesn't read their content or save anything about them.

You can turn notification access off at any time in your phone's settings. BosKira then stops recording payments, and anything you add by hand still works.

What BosKira stores

  • Transactions recorded from those notifications or added by you, with the categories, splits and paybacks you set.
  • The text of the payment notifications themselves, so they can be read again if a later app update understands them better.
  • Your preferences, such as "always put this shop in Food & Drink".

All of this is stored only on your phone, in encrypted databases. The encryption key is held by your phone's secure hardware (Android Keystore), and the app asks for your fingerprint or screen lock when it starts. BosKira's data is excluded from Android backups, so it isn't copied to Google Drive or another phone.

You can make a backup yourself. It's a file locked with a password you choose, saved wherever you pick. If you save it to a cloud service such as Google Drive, that copy is kept by that service. Without the password nobody can open it, and it can't be recovered if you forget the password.

What BosKira sends, and to whom

Nothing. BosKira doesn't request Android's internet permission, so it cannot connect to the internet. There are no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting services and no third-party tracking. We never see your data and can't sell or share it.

The only way information leaves the app is if you choose to share it yourself, for example by using a share or copy button. It then goes only to the app or person you pick.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete individual transactions and captured notifications in the app. Uninstalling BosKira permanently deletes everything it stored.

Children

BosKira isn't intended for children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If a change affects what BosKira reads or where data goes, the app will also tell you before it takes effect.
